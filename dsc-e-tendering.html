GOOD TO KNOW

Get your DSC ready before the tender deadline

E-tendering portals use your Digital Signature Certificate to log in, sign forms and submit bids. Most last-minute failures are avoidable: the wrong certificate type, a missing driver, an unsupported browser or Java setting, or a certificate that was never tested.

Test your token on the bidding computer well before the closing date. If the portal does not detect the certificate, you still have time to fix the driver, browser or certificate registration.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which DSC is required for e-tendering?+

Most government e-procurement portals ask for a Class 3 certificate, often with signing and encryption. Read the portal instructions or tender document, or ask us to confirm.

Why is my DSC not detected on the tender portal?+

Common causes are a missing or outdated token driver, the token not plugged in, an unsupported browser or Java setup, or an expired certificate. We help you check each of these.

When should I test my DSC?+

Test it at least a few days before the deadline, using the same computer and browser you will use to submit the bid.
