GOOD TO KNOW

Prepare your documents before you apply

The documents for a Class 3 DSC depend on whether the certificate is for an individual or an organisation, and on the Certifying Authority. In general you need valid identity proof, address details, an active mobile number and email, and business documents for organisation certificates.

Make sure names match exactly across all documents. Mismatches are the most common reason for delays. Do not send original documents over unsecured channels, and confirm the approved submission method before sharing anything.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What documents are generally needed for a Class 3 DSC?+

Usually identity proof, address details, a working mobile number and email address, and organisation documents if the certificate is for a company or firm. Requirements can vary, so confirm before applying.

Why must my mobile number and email be active?+

They are used for OTPs and updates during verification and certificate issue.

Can I send my documents on WhatsApp?+

Confirm the approved submission method first. Avoid sharing sensitive documents over unsecured channels.
