BEFORE YOU START

Keep these ready.

GeM sets and updates its own requirements, so we confirm the current list with you at the start.

  • PAN and business registration details
  • GSTIN and bank account details
  • Active mobile number and email for OTPs
  • Valid Class 3 DSC with a working USB token
  • Product details, images and specifications for your catalogue
GOOD TO KNOW

Why GeM profile and catalogue quality matter

On GeM, buyers find sellers through catalogues and bids. Incomplete profiles, unclear product details or missing approvals can keep your products from being listed or can lead to rejection.

We help you complete the profile, structure the catalogue and track approvals, then support your first bids. Once registered, you can also use our tender search and bid preparation support.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I register as a seller on GeM?+

You create a seller account on the GeM portal, verify your identity and business details, and complete your profile. We guide you through each step so the information matches your documents.

Do I need a DSC for GeM?+

GeM uses digital signatures for key seller actions, so a valid Class 3 DSC is normally required. We can supply and test one.

Can you upload my product catalogue?+

Yes. We help prepare and upload catalogue entries and follow up on approvals.

How long does approval take?+

Timelines depend on GeM and on how complete your documents and catalogue are, so we cannot promise a fixed duration.
