Where can I get a Class 3 Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) in Chennai? +

PrimeTenders.in is a trusted DSC vendor in Chennai providing Class 3 Signing and Encryption certificates with cryptographic USB tokens, guided paperless video verification, and installation support for GeM, e-tendering, MCA, GST, and Income Tax portals.

Do I need a Signing or Combo (Signing + Encryption) Class 3 DSC for e-tendering? +

Most government e-procurement portals (including GeM, CPPP, and state tender portals) require a Class 3 Combo DSC (both Signing and Encryption) to digitally sign documents and encrypt financial price bids securely.
