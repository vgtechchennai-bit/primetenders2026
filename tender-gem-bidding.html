GOOD TO KNOW

Why bidders lose tenders on technicalities

Many bids are rejected before price is even looked at: a missing document, a mismatched catalogue entry, a wrong file format or a late submission. A structured process of tender search, eligibility check, document preparation and tested submission removes most of these risks.

We work on GeM, CPPP, Indian Railways e-procurement, state e-tender portals such as TN Tenders, and other public sector portals. We can also build a simple tracker so every due date and bid status stays in one place.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you help with GeM seller registration and catalogues?+

Yes. We support seller profile setup, product catalogue uploads and brand or OEM approval processes on GeM.

Which portals do you support for tenders?+

GeM, CPPP, Indian Railways e-procurement, state portals such as TN Tenders and other public sector portals. If you use a different portal, send us the tender link and we will confirm.

Can you guarantee that we will win a tender?+

No one can guarantee an award. We help you find relevant tenders, prepare compliant documents and submit correctly, which improves your chance of being technically qualified.

Do I need a DSC to bid?+

Most e-tender portals require a valid Class 3 DSC. We can supply and test one before your deadline.
