GOOD TO KNOW

Replace manual spreadsheets with a tool your team uses

Many small businesses run on scattered Excel files and copy-paste reports. A custom web app connected to Google Sheets can capture data once, calculate automatically and show live dashboards, without the cost of a full server setup.

Typical projects include tender and order trackers, quotation and PDF generators, MIS dashboards, role-based sales and operations screens, and email or WhatsApp alerts. We start with a small working version so you can test it with your team.
